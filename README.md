AbyteMedix

AbyteMedix is a modern and comprehensive Medical Store & Pharmacy Management System designed to simplify and automate day-to-day operations of pharmacies, medical stores, and healthcare retailers.

The system provides a centralized platform for managing medicines, inventory, purchases, sales, suppliers, customers, stock levels, batches, expiry dates, pricing, payments, and business reporting.

🚀 Key Features
💊 Medicine Management — Manage thousands of medicines with complete product information.
📦 Inventory Management — Real-time stock tracking and inventory control.
🏷️ Batch Management — Track medicine batches, purchase rates, selling rates, and quantities.
⏰ Expiry Management — Monitor expired and near-expiry medicines to reduce stock losses.
🧾 POS / Sales — Fast and efficient billing system for daily medical-store sales.
🛒 Purchase Management — Manage purchases, purchase invoices, suppliers, and stock receiving.
💰 Pricing Management — Handle purchase prices, retail prices, custom rates, and price changes.
👥 Customer Management — Maintain customer records and transaction history.
🏢 Supplier Management — Manage suppliers, balances, purchases, and payment records.
📊 Reports & Analytics — Generate sales, purchase, inventory, profit, stock, and business reports.
💳 Payment & Accounts — Track customer and supplier payments, balances, and transactions.
🔍 Barcode Support — Quickly search and sell medicines using barcode scanning.
🖥️ Offline Desktop Application — Designed to operate reliably without requiring a constant internet connection.
⚡ High-Volume Data Handling — Built with scalability in mind for medical stores with large medicine catalogs and high daily transaction volumes.
🎯 Objective

The goal of AbyteMedix is to provide medical stores with a reliable, fast, and easy-to-use software solution that replaces manual processes and simplifies pharmacy operations.

The system is designed with a focus on performance, accuracy, inventory control, automation, and ease of use, making it suitable for small medical stores as well as larger pharmacy businesses.
